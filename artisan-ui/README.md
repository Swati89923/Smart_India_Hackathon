# Module 02: Artisan Basic UI & Core Workflow (`artisan-ui/`)

## Overview
The **Artisan UI** module houses the client-side user interface and core application flow specifically crafted for rural Indian master artisans and micro-entrepreneurs.

Recognizing that many master craftspersons have limited formal literacy and low digital fluency, the interface adheres to a **Voice-First, Vernacular-First, and Visual-First** design philosophy with minimal text input, prominent voice guidance, and large tactile tap targets.

## Key User Journey & Screens

1. **Artisan Onboarding & Identification**:
   - Frictionless mobile OTP authentication.
   - Profile badge linking with **Pehchan ID** (Ministry of Textiles) and **PM Vishwakarma Yojana**.

2. **The 7-Step Product Onboarding Wizard**:
   - **Step 1: Capture**: Guided multi-angle camera screen with craft alignment overlay.
   - **Step 2: Enhance**: Real-time visual comparison (Before vs After studio enhancement).
   - **Step 3: Voice Prompt**: Dynamic vernacular voice prompt asking the artisan to describe the product in their mother tongue (Hindi, Bhojpuri, Bengali, Tamil, etc.).
   - **Step 4: AI Catalogue Review**: Previewing the bilingual description and emotional story generated by Gemini AI.
   - **Step 5: Fair Price Calculation**: Transparent breakdown showing material costs, hours spent, and recommended fair selling price.
   - **Step 6: ONDC & Multi-Channel Publish**: One-tap publishing to ONDC network, GeM, and social commerce.
   - **Step 7: Connect**: Live enquiry stream showing incoming buyer interest.

3. **Artisan Dashboard**:
   - Catalog management (active listings, draft products, sold items).
   - Real-time inquiry alerts with negotiation counter-offer prompts.
   - Income & fair-trade earnings analytics.
